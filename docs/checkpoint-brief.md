## Project

**Name:** Countercheck

**Idea:** A buyer-side control for agent-to-agent service purchases. Countercheck checks current Concordium Testnet seller registration, Agent Card hash, and registered wallet against a live x402 payment request, applies a fixed native-CCD spend policy, and requires approval of the exact transaction digest before a sponsored Testnet purchase. A local receipt links the payment to the returned service response.

**Problem hypothesis:** A payment can settle while a buyer still lacks a simple, reviewable link between the seller it intended to pay, the registered seller wallet, the x402 terms it approved, and the service result. Current evidence is developer proposals, not buyer interviews or prevalence data.

**Current proof:** One configured local Testnet run completed on 19 November 2026. Sanitized evidence: [`../evidence/live-e2e-2026-11-19.json`](../evidence/live-e2e-2026-11-19.json). Transaction: [CCDScan Testnet link](https://testnet.ccdscan.io/?d=transaction&dsh=71b36427263821b76278df9fce399ea8881b5ad342da95f476e2f5c4dbbb5147). The explorer was not independently inspected during capture. This is one run, not production readiness or market validation.
