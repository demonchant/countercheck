# Ecosystem research

**Initial research:** 11 November 2026. **Source refresh:** 19 November 2026. Findings below distinguish individual issue proposals from validated demand.

## 19 November evidence refresh

- Concordium's current documentation describes CIS-8004 records as active/revoked agent NFTs with an off-chain Agent Card URI and SHA-256 hash, plus an `agentWallet` metadata field. A passing badge check establishes registration/accountability and card integrity, not service quality or safe conduct. See [CIS-8004](https://docs.concordium.com/en/mainnet/technical-reference/agent-registry/cis-8004.html) and [badge verification](https://docs.concordium.com/en/mainnet/technical-reference/agent-registry/concordium-badge.html), retrieved 19 Nov 2026.
- Direct developer/operator reports identify opaque payment integration failures and concern about spend-scope enforcement: [x402 issue #2993](https://github.com/x402-foundation/x402/issues/2993) (discovery description/size failure), [MCP discussion #2436](https://github.com/modelcontextprotocol/modelcontextprotocol/discussions/2436) (opaque 402 failures and settlement checks), and [x402 issue #3500](https://github.com/x402-foundation/x402/issues/3500) (authorization-scope evidence proposal). These are specific reports/discussions, not evidence of frequency or market demand.
- Counter-evidence: [x402-doctor](https://github.com/Fizzl13/x402-doctor) already checks 402 challenges and protocol details without payment. Generic challenge diagnostics are not a gap. Countercheck's narrow proposed distinction is the buyer-side combination of Concordium Registry/card/wallet checks with a local amount/recipient/network policy.
- The 19 Nov live run in [`../evidence/live-demo-preflight-2026-11-19.json`](../evidence/live-demo-preflight-2026-11-19.json) confirms one successful read-only buyer workflow. It does not validate broad user demand.

## Ecosystem map

- **Agent discovery/identity:** MCP exposes tools; A2A Agent Cards describe agents and services. The ERC-8004 draft describes on-chain discovery and trust signals across organizational boundaries, while leaving trust models pluggable. [ERC-8004 specification](https://github.com/erc-8004/erc-8004-contracts/blob/master/ERC8004SPEC.md)
- **Concordium:** CIS-8004 registers an agent as a token, stores a public card pointer/hash and can attach an external key through CIS-8. Registry MCP offers read and transaction-building tools; its hosted service holds no private keys. The badge verifies an active record and card integrity, not endorsement or behavior. [Registry docs](https://docs.concordium.com/en/mainnet/how-to/integrations/agent-registry.html), [MCP docs](https://docs.concordium.com/en/mainnet/technical-reference/agent-registry/mcp-service.html), [badge limits](https://www.concordium.com/article/the-verified-by-concordium-badge-the-trust-mark-that-travels-with-your-agent)
- **Payments:** x402 uses HTTP 402 requirements and a client payment flow; Concordium's integration adds sponsored transactions, where the user/agent signs the transfer and a facilitator sponsors/broadcasts it. Sponsorship pays network fees, not the resource price. [x402 package docs](https://www.npmjs.com/package/%40x402/concordium)
- **Existing alternatives:** generic payment rails establish settlement; ERC-8004 records identity/reputation; registries and discovery tools already exist. Countercheck must add a verifiable policy-enforced sequence, not claim these technologies are absent.

## Regulation boundary

The EU AI Act's logging provisions are scoped to high-risk systems; they do not establish that every agent must use a public chain or that Countercheck's local receipt satisfies legal duties. Use the [current consolidated official text](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/2026-07-27/eng) and obtain legal review for an actual regulated deployment. Countercheck's receipt is an engineering record only, not a compliance attestation.

## Evidence quality and open questions

The issue reports are primary evidence of developers proposing a need, not direct evidence from buyers/operators or evidence of market frequency. Issue #2332 also points to related work, so novelty is bounded and unproven. The official protocol and Registry docs are primary technical evidence. Concordium's ecosystem statements are sponsor-reported. No interviews, customer telemetry, or paid-user data were supplied. Direct user validation is still needed on: who reviews a receipt; which seller identity/payee linkage is expected; what constitutes acceptable delegated scope; whether badge checks improve a decision; and how much audit detail is lawful/necessary.
